# Marketing editorial gate

Use before any of Chandler's copy is forwarded to Marco, placed in a channel, used in a newsletter, Google Doc, Markdown file or other artifact, or relied on as final. This is Darcy's own review; it does not authorise Darcy to rewrite specialist copy.

## Read the real thing

Read the complete copy in its actual intended context, not a summary, excerpt or tool preview. For a continuation, read the user's existing opening and the addition together. For a pack, read every item and its source evidence. Read the current brief, the latest user corrections and the approved version.

## Judge the whole piece

Check:

- Does it continue the user's argument rather than restart or rewrite it?
- Is the opening preserved and advanced?
- Are referents clear and the meaning concrete?
- Is the example relevant to the actual audience and product?
- Is every claim supported by the supplied source, without an implication the source does not establish?
- Does it read as one piece at the requested length and channel?
- Would you recommend it to Marco without qualification?

Chandler's self-review, slop-detector result, preflight receipt or a clean revision never replaces this judgment. A detector or Jev flag can find a pattern; it is advisory, not a mandatory success oracle, and cannot decide whether the piece is worth sending.

## Withhold, correct, re-review

If you would not recommend it, do not forward it. Return the observed defects through the responsible specialist with `rework=true` and the original thread_id. Preserve approved material outside the correction scope. State the defect, not a new creative direction. Allow at most two focused revisions. If the copy still fails, stop the loop: revisit the brief, ask for a materially different approach, or assign a thinker. Do not force weak copy to pass through unlimited churn. Do not present a menu of options or ask Marco to choose between drafts.

A rejected premise stays rejected; do not recycle its framing in a fresh opening. A correction addresses the observed acceptance failure, not an open-ended search for a different style.

## All destinations

The gate applies everywhere the copy will be used: posts, newsletters, Google Docs, Markdown, decks, email drafts and any assembled artifact. A specialist's internal approval or a previous version's approval does not transfer to a changed version. Record what passed and which version was used.

## Verification

The exact copy version forwarded, relied on or released has been read in full context and would be recommended. The user's opening or source is preserved, claims are supported, and any unresolved uncertainty is stated rather than hidden.
